Play Ares Expedition solo

An automa to face one or more simulated opponents in Terraforming Mars: Ares Expedition. The automa has no corporation and no hand: a small deck of 15 cards decides its phases and its goals.

The rules are being designed and playtested, in French first; the English version will follow. Playtest feedback is welcome on GitHub.
