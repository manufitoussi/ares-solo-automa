Terraforming Mars · Ares Expedition

Automa hybrid

An opponent that plays almost like a player.

15 automa cardsVersion 0.4

An automa to play Terraforming Mars: Ares Expedition solo. It takes project cards, raises its production and terraforms Mars; a small deck of 15 cards decides its phases and its targets. It has no hand and asks you to make almost no decisions.
