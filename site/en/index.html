Automa hybrid

An opponent that plays almost like a player.

15 automa cardsVersion 0.6

An automa to play Terraforming Mars: Ares Expedition solo. It builds up its engine, terraforms Mars and scores points; a small deck of 15 cards decides its phases and its targets. Everything runs on cubes: its turn needs no arithmetic and asks the player for almost no decisions.

How it plays

One card per round

While the player picks a phase, the automa flips a card: it gives its phase and its target. Its deck gives it a style, with plenty of Production and Action, and three reactive cards: it strikes back, seizes the moment, or rushes the lagging parameter.

→→+1 step

Cubes, no arithmetic

Every revealed phase earns it one copper, and 5 copper make one silver. Everything costs 1 silver: an oxygen or temperature step, an ocean or an engine step. Its turn takes seconds, without counting a single MC.

IDevelopment Automa09 / 15 +1 , then 1 : engine +1. Ocean1 Player plays 1 green card.

An engine that speeds up

In Development, it invests one silver in its engine; in Production, its engine pays back that many copper. Slow at first, it gains power as the game goes on, like a real player. But every engine step costs one silver that does not terraform.

?Course Automa15 / 15 Action, on the least advanced parameter. Least advanced Player uses its actions.

It races the player for Mars

It raises oxygen, temperature and oceans, gains TR and scores VP in Construction. It places no forests. It terraforms alongside the player but plays to finish ahead, and it can end the game before the player is ready.

02

Versus the official solo

The base game's solo mode is a race against the clock. The hybrid automa turns it into a duel: a rival that terraforms for itself and scores points.

Official soloHybrid automa
The goalTerraform Mars alone before the 25 rounds are over: win or lose.Finish ahead of an opponent that scores points.
The 2nd phaseA neutral deck of 5 phases, each once every 5 rounds: you can guess it.A 15-card deck, with more Production and Action, and reactive cards: you read it rather than guess it.
TerraformingDepending on the level, a boost to oxygen or temperature, worth no points to anyone.It terraforms for itself: every step it takes scores it a point and is one step fewer for the player.
Game length25 rounds, always.Until Mars is terraformed: the stronger it is, the shorter the game.
The player's choicesThe chosen phase only helps the player.Production and Development also speed it up: every phase choice is a trade-off.

What you need

With the printed cards

  • The base game Terraforming Mars: Ares Expedition, with a player board and copper and silver cubes for the automa.
  • The 15 automa cards: 3 A4 sheets, with their backs and 3 optional reference cards.
  • Optionally, the player phase cards: 1 A4 sheet (5 cards), instead of the game's phase cards.
  • The rulebook, for the first game.

Or with the app

  • The base game, with its usual phase cards.
  • A phone, a tablet or a computer: the app draws the automa cards, counts its cubes and tells what to do on its board.
  • Nothing to print.

Why player phase cards?

They are optional and replace the game's phase cards. For the player, they say the same thing in our own words: the ability and the bonus. On top of that, each one shows what the automa does when the phase is revealed. During the game, everything is on the table: no need to open the rulebook.
